5. COMPANY-WIDE SALES, CREDIT, STOCK, AND NUMBERING SETTINGS
===============================================================================

30. Added General Sales Discount Percentage.
    - Configurable from Company Settings.
    - Validated between 0% and 100%.
    - Applied to manual sales when no explicit document discount is entered.
    - Applied to public storefront product prices.
    - Applied again during backend online checkout calculation so the displayed
      price and final charged price cannot differ.

31. Added Maximum Customer Debt.
    - Defaults to 300,000.
    - Configurable at company level.
    - Individual customers may override the company credit limit.
    - A manual sale is blocked if it would exceed the customer's allowed debt.

32. Added Default Debt Due Days.
    - Defaults to 30 days.
    - Configurable at company level.
    - Individual customers may override the due period.
    - New unpaid manual-sale balances receive an automatic due date.

33. Added Allow Negative Stock Sales.
    - Configurable in Company Settings.
    - When disabled, insufficient stock blocks the sale.
    - When enabled, a manual invoice can sell an existing catalog product even if
      the current quantity is zero or insufficient.

34. Added configurable automatic purchase numbering.
    - Purchase prefix.
    - Next purchase number.
    - Purchase-number increment.
    - Numbers use a consistent padded format.

35. Added configurable automatic sale numbering.
    - Sale prefix.
    - Next sale number.
    - Sale-number increment.
    - Numbers use a consistent padded format.

36. Made automatic numbering concurrency-safe.
    - Company settings are locked while the next document number is assigned.
    - Number advancement and document creation occur in the same transaction.
    - A failed transaction does not permanently consume the number.

    - Supports company-style discounts such as 10% + 5%.
    - The second percentage applies after the first percentage.
    - Available for purchase lines and manual-sale lines.

    - Available for purchase and manual-sale totals.
    - Existing fixed discount fields remain supported.
    - The effective saved discount reflects line, document, and fixed discounts.

    - Line net totals update from quantity, price/cost, and both percentages.
    - Purchase and sale settlement summaries use the same stacked-discount math.
    - Profit preview remains available for manual sales.

43. Added negative-stock sale reconciliation.
    - A negative inventory balance represents stock sold before purchase.
    - A later purchase first clears the negative deficit.
    - Only the remaining purchased quantity becomes a new sellable inventory lot.
    - This prevents lot quantities from exceeding physical inventory after a
      backfilled purchase.

44. Preserved lot and expiry controls.
    - FEFO consumption remains used for available stock.
    - Expired stock cannot be received as normal sellable stock.
    - Expired stock cannot be sold as available stock.
    - Lot movement history remains available from the sale list.

45. Improved customer settlement in manual sales.
    - Selected customer debt, credit, credit limit, and overdue state are shown.
    - Existing account credit can be applied automatically.
    - Due dates are captured for unpaid balances.
    - Walk-in customers cannot create an overpayment credit.
